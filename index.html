How to use the board

Cards

  • Double-click the board or press + Card to add one
  • Click the arrow to unfold a card; cards below move down
  • Click any text and type where you clicked
  • Drag a card's header to move it
  • Drag the corner to make a card wider
  • Cmd/Ctrl + Enter or Esc finishes typing

Linking and selecting

  • Drag a side dot onto another card to link them, or onto empty board for a new linked card
  • Drag on empty board to select several; Shift-click to add
  • ⌘C / ⌘V copies and pastes cards; ⌘Z undoes

Adding things

  • Paste pictures, links or text onto the board
  • Text (T) adds a heading; Shift + Enter for a new line
  • Marker (M) draws; Apple Pencil draws straight away
  • Timeline shows week, month or year columns

Moving around

  • Scroll or Space-drag to pan
  • Pinch or Ctrl/⌘ + scroll to zoom
  • Organise tidies the board; Fit shows everything
